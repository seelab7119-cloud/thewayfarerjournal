Rabat and Salé: a two-day guide for 2027

Two cities, one river. How to see the Kasbah, the Hassan Tower, Chellah and Salé’s old medina without a car.

Verified 20 Nov 2026

Rabat is on Lonely Planet’s list of places to visit in 2027, and its historic core has been a UNESCO World Heritage site since 2012. Salé faces it across the Bouregreg river. Together they make an easy, walkable trip of two days.

  1. Day 1, morning: Kasbah of the UdayasA 12th-century Almohad citadel on the cliff where the river meets the Atlantic. Walk its white-and-blue lanes, then down to the beach below.
  2. Day 1, late morning: the MedinaRabat’s old town sits next to the Kasbah. Good for crafts, street food and a calm market walk.
  3. Day 1, afternoon: Hassan Tower and Mohammed V MausoleumThe tower is what remains of a vast mosque begun in the 12th century and never finished. The royal mausoleum stands on the same esplanade.
  4. Day 1, evening: the Royal TheatreZaha Hadid’s theatre on the riverbank gave its opening performance on 22 April 2026. It has an 1,800-seat hall and a 7,000-seat open-air amphitheatre; check its programme for shows.
  5. Day 2, morning: ChellahRoman ruins and a Merinid necropolis in gardens full of storks, reopened after restoration. Book on the official site.
  6. Day 2, afternoon: cross to SaléTake the tram over the Hassan II bridge, or a flouka rowing boat across the river. Salé’s medina is quieter than Rabat’s, with its Great Mosque and the old sea gate, Bab Mrisa.
