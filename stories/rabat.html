Rabat makes Lonely Planet’s list of places to visit in 2027

Morocco’s capital joins Québec, the Veneto and Oman among the 25 destinations in Best in Travel 2027.

Verified 20 Nov 2026

Rabat is one of 25 destinations in Lonely Planet’s Best in Travel 2027, released in late September 2026. The list does not rank its picks. The publisher singles out the city’s historic sites, Atlantic beaches, cultural scene and traditional riads.

It is a second year in the spotlight: Time Out recently named Rabat’s beachside L’Océan district the world’s second-coolest neighbourhood. Oman is also on the 2027 list, and Abu Dhabi features among the guide’s recommendations.

Getting around

Rabat and its sister city Salé face each other across the Bouregreg river. Cross by tram, grand taxi, or in a shared wooden rowing boat. Morocco welcomed 15.7 million visitors in the first nine months of 2026, so book riads early for spring and autumn.
