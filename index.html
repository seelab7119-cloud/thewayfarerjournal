How to apply for a fully funded scholarship

Guides desk
  1. Shortlist from official pagesPick three to five programmes. Check nationality, age, degree and work-experience rules on the funder's own site before anything else.
  2. Map the routeMany awards need two applications: one for university admission and one for the scholarship. Note both deadlines and any earlier university cut-off.
  3. Build the file earlyPassport, transcripts with certified translations, CV, language test and referees. Ask referees at least six weeks ahead.
  4. Write to the criteriaFunders publish what they score: leadership, academic merit, impact at home. Answer each one with a concrete example.
  5. Submit days earlyPortals slow down near deadlines. Save every confirmation email and the time zone of the cut-off.
  6. Prepare for the next roundShortlists, interviews and results follow months later. Put those dates in your calendar now.
Never pay for a "guaranteed" scholarship. Government programmes such as Chevening, Gates Cambridge, Eiffel and Türkiye Bursları take applications through their own portals or partner universities. Anyone selling a place, or charging to "submit" for you, is not part of the process.

Our standards

Deadlines, amounts and eligibility rules come only from the awarding body's own page, and each listing shows when we last checked it. Dates not yet announced are marked Expected. Aggregator sites are used to find leads, never as proof.

Every visa story is complete: who needs it and who is exempt, the documents, every fee, processing time, validity and length of stay, how and where to apply, what happens if you are refused, and the official links. We do not publish part of the picture.

We correct errors within 24 hours and note the change at the foot of the story. Nothing is quietly deleted.
